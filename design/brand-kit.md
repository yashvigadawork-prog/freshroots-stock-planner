# Freshroots Stock Planner: brand kit

The look: warm, calm and friendly, like a good grocery shelf. Rooty the root-vegetable mascot keeps the mood light, but the numbers stay serious.

## Colour
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | #FBF6E9 | Page background |
| Paper | #FFFDF7 | Cards |
| Ink | #1F2A24 | Body text |
| Deep green | #173D2D | Headings, header |
| Leaf green | #256B47 | Buttons, links, "OK" |
| Mint | #E3F0E6 | Soft green fills |
| Harvest gold | #E8A628 | Accents, warnings |
| Butter | #FBEBC6 | Overstock fill, amber warning |
| Terracotta | #A7351E | Stockout risk |
| Plum | #583B78 | High demand |

Status is never colour alone. Every status shows a coloured dot AND a word.

## Type
- Headings: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body: DM Sans
- Nothing smaller than 13px. Buttons are at least 44px tall.

## Mascot
Rooty (`rooty.svg`) appears only on Home and All clear. He bobs gently.

## Icons
One line icon per product type in `icons/`: beverage, snack, staple, dairy alt, condiment. 24px grid, 1.75 stroke, deep green.

## Motion
- Cards rise in when a page loads.
- Rooty bobs.
- Only the stockout dot pulses.
- All motion is switched off when the device asks for reduced motion.
